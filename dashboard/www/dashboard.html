RX frequency calibration, per mode. The modem measures how far the received signal is from the receive frequency of a mode; the offset is added to the receive frequency of that mode only.
  1. Simplex: set Mode to Simplex on the Modem tab and press Save. A duplex hotspot repeats what it hears, so its transmitter is keyed while you transmit and it cannot be measured.
  2. Service: start the protocol service of the mode. Its row appears below once it has registered.
  3. Reference: use a transmitter with an accurate frequency on the receive frequency of the mode (normal modulation of that mode or a plain carrier), at a level of -50 to -100 dBm at the hotspot. The level is shown in the result.
  4. Key the transmitter first, then press Measure or Calibrate, and keep it keyed until the result is shown. Measure takes a few seconds, Calibrate up to about 30 seconds. If your radio has a time-out timer, key again before it ends. De-key after the result.
  5. Measure only shows the error. Calibrate measures, saves the offset and measures again until the error is small. Save stores an offset you typed, Reset sets it to 0.
  6. When you are done, set Mode back to Duplex if you use it.
The modem is set to Duplex: the hotspot repeats your transmission, and the measurement is refused while its transmitter is keyed ("the transmitter is keyed"). Set Mode to Simplex on the Modem tab and press Save first.
